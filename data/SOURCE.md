# Dataset: stories by Ömer Seyfettin

`omer_seyfettin.txt` holds 71 short stories and short texts by the Turkish writer Ömer Seyfettin (1884–1920). It is the first, small training text of this project.

## Licence

Ömer Seyfettin died in 1920. Article 27 of the Turkish Law on Intellectual and Artistic Works (Law No. 5846, FSEK) protects a work while its author is alive and for 70 years after the author's death. The same 70-year term applies to works first published after the author's death. The protection of these texts therefore ended long ago, and the texts are in the public domain in Turkey.

- Law text: <https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.3.5846.pdf>

Six short pages name edebice.net as their online source: "Aşk", "Bahar Rüzgârı", "Hicrân-ı Müzmin", "Mefkure", "Yalnızlık" and "Yıkık Han". If a page was copied from a modern simplified edition, the person who simplified the text may hold rights in that version.

## Format

- Each story starts with its title on one line, followed by an empty line and the text.
- Two empty lines separate one story from the next. The stories are sorted by title.
- A line that holds only `*` marks a section break inside a story.

## What was changed

1. The pages "Nadan" and "Keramet" only redirect to "Nâdan" and "Kerâmet", so the redirect targets were used.
2. Wikisource markup was removed: templates, footnotes, the "Dipnotlar" and "Kaynakça" sections, categories, links, HTML tags, bold and italic marks, and web links.
3. Decorative separators (`🙝🙟`, `±`) were replaced with a `*` line. Invisible soft hyphens were removed. Quotes written as ``` ``text´´ ``` and `` `text´ `` were replaced with “text” and ‘text’.
4. The text was normalised to Unicode NFC, so that a letter such as `ş` is always stored as a single character.

The scripts that produced this file are not in the repository yet.
